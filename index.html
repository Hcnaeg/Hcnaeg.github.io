Research

Multimodal adaptation

I adapt multimodal models to the task at hand: selecting visual evidence by its estimated downstream utility beyond semantic relevance, using a training-free, lightweight surrogate, and fine-tuning pretrained vision-language models to the image resolution each task needs. (ACL 2026, EMNLP 2025)

Test-time and
post-training learning

Models can keep improving after training by learning at test time from self-generated supervision, but that supervision can reinforce its own bias. My structured exploration framework combines multi-view reasoning with instance-adaptive sampling to mitigate this bias, improving reasoning performance while reducing sampling cost by over 30%. (under review)

Model interpretability

I also study how evidence and context shape a model's answer: tracing evidence across semantic input regions in vision-language models, and analyzing bounded responses to repeated assertions and the sparse pathways that mediate contextual override. (ICML 2026, EMNLP 2026, under review)

Earlier work brought external knowledge into recommendation and dataset retrieval, including TRAWL, deployed in WeChat Subscriptions (CIKM 2025, Findings of EMNLP 2023).
